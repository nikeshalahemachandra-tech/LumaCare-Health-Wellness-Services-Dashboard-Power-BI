LumaCare Health & Wellness Services Dashboard – Power BI

Developed an interactive **Power BI dashboard** to analyze LumaCare Health & Wellness Services performance across branches, service categories, booking channels, and customer segments.

The dashboard provides key insights into **revenue, cost, profit, profit margin, service units, transactions, and customer satisfaction** using DAX measures and interactive visualizations. It includes KPI cards, monthly revenue and profit trends, branch performance, service category analysis, top service packages, booking channel analysis, customer segment comparisons, and interactive slicers.

# Key Features

* Interactive KPI cards for Revenue, Profit, Profit Margin, and Satisfaction Score
* Monthly Revenue & Profit trend analysis
* Revenue comparison by branch
* Profit Margin analysis by service category
* Top 5 service packages by revenue
* Booking channel performance analysis
* Customer segment analysis
* Interactive Date, Branch, Service Category, and Booking Channel slicers
* DAX measures for business performance analysis

# Tools & Technologies

**Power BI | DAX | Data Cleaning | Data Visualization | Business Intelligence | Data Analysis**

# Project Objective

To transform healthcare and wellness service transaction data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that supports performance monitoring and data-driven decision-making.
